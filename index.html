02THE RULESPUBLISHED

FOUR RULES. NO EXCEPTIONS.

  1. 01

    Fees go to the vault

    Every $SHARE trade on pump.fun pays a creator fee. All of it goes to one public vault wallet.

  2. 02

    Every hour, at :00 UTC

    The vault swaps that hour's fees into SPCX on Jupiter. One buy per hour, on the hour.

  3. 03

    The SPCX is never sold

    What goes in stays in. There is no sell, no rebalance, no exit.

  4. 04

    Anyone can watch

    The vault is a normal Solana wallet. Every buy is a public transaction you can check yourself.
